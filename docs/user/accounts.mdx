Accounts are where your money physically lives; the budget is the plan laid over them. Budgero keeps that distinction sharp — which is why picking the right account type matters more than it first appears.

**On-budget** accounts feed your budget: their money shows up in [Ready to Assign](/docs/ready-to-assign) and their spending hits categories. **Off-budget** (tracking) accounts contribute to net worth but stay out of the envelope math — you don't budget your retirement fund's market swings.

Credit cards and loans are **flexible**: on-budget if you want Budgero's debt machinery managing payments and payoff (see [Debt tracking](/docs/debt-tracking)), off-budget if you only want the balance tracked.

## Starting balances

When you create an account with a balance, Budgero records it as an opening transaction so the books balance from day one:

- **Asset accounts** get an inflow ("Initial Balance") that lands in Income — meaning your existing money becomes assignable immediately.
- **Debt accounts** can record both the total borrowed and what you've already paid, so the balance reflects what you actually owe today.

## Multi-currency accounts

Any account can be denominated in over 120 fiat currencies or 25 major cryptocurrencies, independent of your [budget currency](/docs/budget-currency). For a foreign-currency account, each transaction stores two amounts: the **original** (what actually happened, in the account's currency) and the **converted** value (what your budget sees), along with the exchange rate used.

Rates resolve in a defined order:

1. **Custom rates** you've pinned to a date range — useful when you moved money at a known rate.
2. **Fetched rates** — Budgero retrieves daily rates automatically.
3. **Manual rates** you've entered — these also make multi-currency work fully offline and on self-hosted setups without a rate API.
4. **Nearest recent rate** — the closest earlier day's rate (up to a week back) when the exact day isn't cached.

You can also override the rate on any individual transaction — the right tool when your card's actual settlement rate differs from the market average and you want the converted amount to match your statement to the cent.

## Transfers between your accounts

Add a transfer by picking **Transfer: &lt;account&gt;** as the payee. Budgero creates both sides, one in each account, linked together.

When both sides are already there (bank sync or a file import brought in the money leaving one account and arriving in the other), change the payee of one of them to **Transfer: &lt;account&gt;**. Budgero looks in the other account for a matching transaction in the opposite direction (same amount, within the window you set under **Settings → Duplicate transactions**) and offers to use it as the other side instead of adding a new one. Each side keeps its own date and amount, so both accounts still match your bank. Across currencies the received amount is the one your bank actually booked.

## Time of day

A transaction can carry an optional time next to its date. It's empty unless you set it, and it changes nothing about balances, budgets or months: those follow the date alone.

- **Entering a time.** The add-transaction dialog and the mobile transaction details have a time field next to the date. Leave it empty if you don't care about times.
- **Seeing times in the register.** Turn on **Settings → Appearance → Show transaction times** to show the time under each date. With it on, the inline entry row has a time field too, and you can click a time in the register to edit it.
- **Order within a day.** With times shown and **Order within a day** set to newest first, each day runs latest time first; transactions without a time sit at the bottom of their day. Ordering by amount ignores times.
- **Transfers** share one time on both sides, and split lines that move money to another account take the time of their transaction. Recurring transactions post without a time.
- **Imports and bank sync** keep the time only while **Show transaction times** is on, on the device that runs the import or sync, and only when the file or bank sends a real one: a time column or a date-time in a CSV (the Time column can be mapped once the setting is on), OFX timestamps, CAMT.053 booking date-times, and bank feeds that include a time. A time of exactly 00:00:00 or 12:00:00, or one time shared by every row of a file or bank fetch, is treated as "no time". Rows imported while the setting was off keep no time. Duplicate detection still matches by date and amount only.
- The **Push API** accepts an optional `time` (`HH:MM`, 24-hour) when adding or updating a transaction. An invalid time is dropped; the transaction is still added.
- **Update every device.** A transaction that carries a time needs this version of Budgero on all your devices: an older app stops syncing with an "update required" message when it meets one, instead of losing the time. Transactions without a time keep syncing to older apps as before.

Times are stored as you see them (`HH:MM`, your local clock), with no time zone conversion.

## Sorting the register

Click a column header in an account register or in All Transactions to sort by it: the first click sorts ascending (oldest date, smallest amount, A to Z), the second descending, and the third goes back to the usual date order. Sorting covers everything your search and filters show, not just the current page. Split transactions stay one row, and upcoming recurring transactions stay together at the top. While a column sort is on, the Balance column shows "—", because a running balance only adds up in date order. On a phone, use **Sort by** above the list. Each register remembers its own sort, on all your devices.

## Reconciliation

Reconciling is the habit that keeps a budget trustworthy, whether you type transactions in or sync them: confirming that Budgero agrees with your bank.

Every transaction has a status. New transactions you enter start **uncleared**; tap the **C** in the register once the transaction shows up in your bank, and it becomes **cleared**. Bank-file imports arrive cleared, and so do posted transactions from [bank sync](/docs/bank-sync-enable-banking). The account header shows your **Cleared** balance next to the full balance — the cleared one is what your bank should show.

1. Open the account and mark the transactions your bank has already posted as cleared.
2. Click **Reconcile** and enter the balance your bank shows. Budgero compares it with the cleared balance.
3. If they differ, find the gap — usually a missed transaction, a duplicate, or one not yet marked cleared. For a small discrepancy that isn't worth the archaeology, Budgero can add an adjustment transaction.
4. Confirm — Budgero locks the cleared transactions up to today as **reconciled** and stamps the account with the reconciliation time. Uncleared transactions stay open for next time.

Changing the amount of a reconciled transaction asks first, then marks it uncleared so it shows up again in your next reconciliation.

Reconciled transactions are your verified history; the stamp tells you how fresh that verification is. Weekly reconciliation takes about two minutes per account. Monthly works too. "Never" is how manual budgets die — see [Ready to Assign](/docs/ready-to-assign) for how unreconciled drift shows up in your numbers.

## Closing an account

Accounts are **archived**, not deleted. An archived account disappears from pickers and day-to-day lists, but every transaction it ever held stays in your reports and history — close your old bank account without losing five years of records. Archiving is reversible; unarchive it and it returns exactly as it was.

Before archiving, bring the balance to zero (transfer remaining funds to another account) so your net worth and Ready to Assign aren't carrying a ghost balance.
