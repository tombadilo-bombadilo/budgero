Every account register and **All Transactions** has a search box above the table. Type what you're looking for — `groceries last month`, `outflows over 100`, `uncleared` — and Budgero turns the words it recognises into filters. Each recognised filter shows up as a chip under the box; click its **×** to drop it. The words left over are searched as plain text.

Not sure what to type? Select the **(i)** at the right of the search box. It explains the search in a few lines and has examples you can try with one click, plus a link to this page.

All filters combine, and every one of them has to match: `Dining out last month over 30` shows Dining out transactions from last month above 30.

The filter words are English for now, whatever language the app is in. Upper and lower case never matter: `Last Month`, `LAST MONTH` and `last month` are the same.

## Suggestions while you type

As you type a name, a list under the box suggests matching **categories, labels, payees and accounts**. Each line says what kind of name it is. Use the arrow keys and <Keys>Enter</Keys> (or <Keys>Tab</Keys>), or click, to pick one.

Picking a suggestion replaces the word you were typing with the full name, with its field and quotes added for you:

- `Hra` + pick the category "Hrana i Piće" → `category:"Hrana i Piće"`
- `-Hra` + pick the same category → `-category:"Hrana i Piće"` (left out)
- `amaz` + pick the payee "Amazon" → `payee:Amazon`

Suggestions also work after a field prefix, even before you type anything: `category:`, `-category:`, `label:`, `-label:`, `payee:` and `account:` list the names of that kind only. They also work inside an open quote (`-"Hrana i`), after `not`, and in the middle of a search if you move the cursor back. Without a prefix, type at least two letters. Accents don't matter: `pice` finds "Piće". Account names are suggested on All Transactions only.

The heading of the list tells you what a pick does: "show only its transactions", or "hide its transactions" after a minus or `not`.

## Plain text

Words the search doesn't recognise are matched against the **payee, memo, category, label, and account** of each transaction, ignoring upper and lower case. Each word is matched on its own and **every word has to appear** somewhere: `corner shop` finds "Corner Shop" and also "Shop on the corner", but not a transaction that only says "shop". A word only has to be part of a field: `shop` also finds "Workshop".

- **Exact phrase:** put words in double quotes to find them together, in that order: `"corner shop"`. Single quotes work too: `'corner shop'`. A quote you haven't closed yet runs to the end of the search.
- **Payees of transfers and splits:** a transfer is also found by the account on the other side (`savings`), and a split by the payees of its lines.
- Plain words show as "Searching: …" under the box rather than as chips.

Values with spaces go in quotes: `payee:"Whole Foods"`, `label:"Work trip"`, `category:"Hrana i Piće"`. Picking a suggestion adds the quotes for you. Field names can be written in any case (`Payee:`). A word with any other colon, such as `http://…` or `re:`, is searched as plain text.

The chip tells you which filter you got: **Category: "Groceries"** is that exact category, **Category contains "groc"** is every category with "groc" in its name. Payee, memo and account chips always mean "contains".

You don't have to type the quotes yourself: type `-` and the start of the name, then pick it from the suggestions.

Some details:

- A minus in front of a number isn't a negation: `-5` is searched as text, and a minus on its own is ignored.
- `not` in front of a date or an amount is just a word: `not last month` doesn't leave out last month (use `before` or `since` instead).
- Scheduled rows (upcoming) have no cleared status, so they never match a status filter, not even `not uncleared`.

How dates combine:

- `since`, `after`, `before` and `between` take any of the dates above: a day, a month, a year, or a relative one like `last month`.
- `since`, `after` and `before` narrow each other and a plain date: `since 2026 before june` is January to May 2026, `2025 after 2025-06` is July to December 2025.
- If you type two plain dates, the last one wins.
- `this week`, `this month` and `this year` end today. Use `next week` or `next 30 days` to see what's scheduled.
- `since` or `after` a future date runs to the end of that day.

A date also moves the register's date range at the top of the page, so the table loads the right period. Removing the date chip keeps that range; change it with the date picker. Weeks start on the day set in **Settings → Budget Settings → First day of the week**.

### Words that look like dates

Month names (including short ones like `mar` or `may`) and four-digit years from 1970 to 2099 are always read as dates. To search for them as text, for example a payee called "May" or "2025" in a memo, put them in quotes: `"may"`, `"2025"`, or use a field: `payee:may`. Anything in quotes is never read as a date, amount, or keyword.

## Money in, money out, transfers

- `inflows`, `inflow`, or `income` — money coming in (transfers excluded).
- `outflows`, `outflow`, `expenses`, `expense`, or `spending` — money going out (transfers excluded).
- `transfers` or `transfer` — only transfers between your accounts.

They can also be written with `is:` (`is:transfer`, `is:income`) and left out with a minus (`-transfers`). If you type two of them, the last one wins.

## Cleared status

- `cleared` — cleared transactions, including reconciled ones.
- `uncleared` — transactions you haven't cleared yet.
- `reconciled` — only reconciled transactions.

You can also write them as `is:cleared`, `is:uncleared`, and `is:reconciled`. Scheduled recurring rows (the projected ones) never match a status, and they don't match `not uncleared` either.

## Amounts

Search by amount with a comparison word or symbol in front of the number:

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>You type</th>
      <th>Finds amounts</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td><code>over 50</code>, <code>above 50</code>, <code>more than 50</code>, <code>&gt;50</code>, <code>&gt; 50</code></td><td>Greater than 50</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>under 20</code>, <code>below 20</code>, <code>less than 20</code>, <code>&lt;20</code>, <code>&lt; 20</code></td><td>Less than 20 (zero-amount rows are left out)</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>exactly 42.50</code>, <code>equal 42.50</code>, <code>equals 42.50</code>, <code>equal to 42.50</code>, <code>=42.50</code>, <code>= 42.50</code></td><td>42.50 (to the cent)</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>between 20 and 50</code> (or <code>between 50 and 20</code>)</td><td>From 20 to 50, both included</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

- **Ranges:** `between 20 and 50`, or combine both ends: `over 20 under 50`. Every amount filter you type has to match.
- **Direction doesn't matter:** an amount filter compares the size of the transaction, whether it's money in or out. Add `outflows` or `inflows` to narrow it down.
- **Currency:** amounts are compared in the currency the table shows. In an account register that is the account's currency when the toggle above the table says **Account Currency**, and the budget currency when it says **Budget Currency**. All Transactions always shows the budget currency. The amount chip names that currency, for example **Over €200.00**.
- **Currency signs:** a currency symbol before or after the number is fine: `over €100`, `over 100€`, `over 100 €`, `<$50`. Currency codes like EUR are not read as part of the amount.
- **Number format:** a dot or a comma works for decimals, and dots, commas, spaces or apostrophes for thousands: `=1250.50`, `=1.250,50`, `=1,250.50`, `=1 250,50` and `=1'250.50` all find 1,250.50. When a number has a single separator followed by exactly three digits (`1.000` or `1,000`), Budgero reads it the way your budget's number format does: as a decimal if that's its decimal mark, as thousands otherwise.
- **A number on its own is text,** not an amount: `42.50` looks for "42.50" in memos and payees. Use `=42.50` to find the amount. `more 50` (without "than") is text too.
- **Years and amounts:** `between 2020 and 2025` is read as years. For amounts that look like years, add a currency sign: `between $2020 and $2025`.

## Categories and labels

- Type a **category name** to show only that category: `groceries`. Names with spaces go in quotes, `"Dining out"`, or use `category:"Dining out"`. A quoted name that matches a category selects that category rather than searching the phrase.
- Use `label:` for **labels**: `label:Vacation`, or `label:"Work trip"` for names with spaces. A label name without `label:` is searched as plain text (which also looks in labels).
- Add several categories (or several labels) to see transactions in **any** of them: `groceries "Dining out"`.
- The easiest way: type the start of the name and pick it from the suggestions. Budgero adds `category:` or `label:` and the quotes.

## Quick filters

Next to the search, the **Uncleared** and **Uncategorized** buttons show how many such transactions there are and filter to them in one click. On an account page, <Keys>Shift+C</Keys> and <Keys>Shift+U</Keys> toggle them from the keyboard — see [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts).
