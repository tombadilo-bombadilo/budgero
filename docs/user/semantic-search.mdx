Every account register and **All Transactions** has a search box above the table. Type what you're looking for — `groceries last month`, `outflows over 100`, `uncleared` — and Budgero turns the words it recognises into filters. Each recognised filter shows up as a chip under the box; click its **×** to drop it. The words left over are searched as plain text.

Not sure what to type? Select the **(i)** at the right of the search box. It explains the search in a few lines and has examples you can try with one click, plus a link to this page.

All filters combine, and every one of them has to match: `Dining out last month over 30` shows Dining out transactions from last month above 30.

The filter words are English for now, whatever language the app is in.

## Plain text

Words the search doesn't recognise are matched against the **payee, memo, category, label, and account** of each transaction, ignoring upper and lower case. Each word is matched on its own and **every word has to appear** somewhere: `corner shop` finds "Corner Shop" and also "Shop on the corner", but not a transaction that only says "shop".

- **Exact phrase:** put words in double quotes to find them together, in that order: `"corner shop"`.
- **Payees of transfers and splits:** a transfer is also found by the account on the other side (`savings`), and a split by the payees of its lines.

Values with spaces go in quotes: `payee:"Whole Foods"`, `label:"Work trip"`.

## Leaving things out

Put a minus in front of anything to leave it out, or write `not` before it:

- `-amazon` hides transactions that mention "amazon" anywhere; `-"gift card"` hides an exact phrase.
- `-payee:amazon`, `-memo:refund`, `-category:rent`, `-label:trip` leave out one field's matches.
- `-groceries` or `not groceries` (a category name) hides that category.
- Keywords work too: `-transfers`, `not uncleared`, `-split`, `not upcoming`.

`groceries -payee:costco` shows groceries bought anywhere except Costco.

A minus in front of a number isn't a negation: `-5` is searched as text.

`since`, `after`, and `before` take any of the dates above, and they narrow each other: `since 2026 before june` is January to May. If you type two plain dates, the last one wins.

A date also moves the register's date range at the top of the page, so the table loads the right period. Removing the date chip keeps that range; change it with the date picker. Weeks start on the day set in **Settings → Budget Settings → First day of the week**.

### Words that look like dates

Month names (including short ones like `mar` or `may`) and four-digit years from 1970 to 2099 are always read as dates. To search for them as text, for example a payee called "May" or "2025" in a memo, put them in quotes: `"may"`, `"2025"`, or use a field: `payee:may`. Anything in quotes is never read as a date, amount, or keyword.

## Money in, money out, transfers

- `inflows`, `inflow`, or `income` — money coming in (transfers excluded).
- `outflows`, `outflow`, `expenses`, `expense`, or `spending` — money going out (transfers excluded).
- `transfers` or `transfer` — only transfers between your accounts.

## Cleared status

- `cleared` — cleared transactions, including reconciled ones.
- `uncleared` — transactions you haven't cleared yet.
- `reconciled` — only reconciled transactions.

You can also write them as `is:cleared`, `is:uncleared`, and `is:reconciled`. Scheduled recurring rows (the projected ones) never match a status, and they don't match `not uncleared` either.

## Amounts

Search by amount with a comparison word or symbol in front of the number:

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>You type</th>
      <th>Finds amounts</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td><code>over 50</code>, <code>above 50</code>, <code>more than 50</code>, <code>&gt;50</code></td><td>Greater than 50</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>under 20</code>, <code>below 20</code>, <code>less than 20</code>, <code>&lt;20</code></td><td>Less than 20</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>exactly 42.50</code>, <code>equal 42.50</code>, <code>=42.50</code></td><td>42.50 (to the cent)</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>between 20 and 50</code></td><td>From 20 to 50, both included</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

- **Ranges:** `between 20 and 50`, or combine both ends: `over 20 under 50`.
- **Direction doesn't matter:** an amount filter compares the size of the transaction, whether it's money in or out. Add `outflows` or `inflows` to narrow it down.
- **Currency:** amounts are compared in the currency the register shows (account or budget currency, from the toggle above the table). A currency sign before or after the number is fine: `over €100`, `over 100 €`.
- **Number format:** a dot or a comma works for decimals, and dots, commas, spaces or apostrophes for thousands: `=1250.50`, `=1.250,50`, `=1,250.50`, and `=1 250,50` all find 1,250.50. When a number has a single separator followed by exactly three digits (`1.000` or `1,000`), Budgero reads it the way your budget's number format does: as a decimal if that's its decimal mark, as thousands otherwise.
- **A number on its own is text,** not an amount: `42.50` looks for "42.50" in memos and payees. Use `=42.50` to find the amount.
- **Years and amounts:** `between 2020 and 2025` is read as years. For amounts that look like years, add a currency sign: `between $2020 and $2025`.

## Categories and labels

- Type a **category name** to show only that category. Names with spaces go in quotes: `"Dining out"`. While you type, Budgero suggests matching categories; pick one to add it. Add several categories to see any of them.
- Use `label:` for **labels**: `label:Vacation`, or `label:"Work trip"` for names with spaces. Several labels show any of them.

## Quick filters

Next to the search, the **Uncleared** and **Uncategorized** buttons show how many such transactions there are and filter to them in one click. On an account page, <Keys>Shift+C</Keys> and <Keys>Shift+U</Keys> toggle them from the keyboard — see [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts).
